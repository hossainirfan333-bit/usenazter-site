Anti Spam Policy

usenazter.com does not permit the use of its domain or email accounts for spam, phishing, malware, deceptive email, purchased mailing lists, unauthorized bulk email, or email sent on behalf of unauthorized third parties.

All users of the domain must comply with applicable email laws, recipient opt out requests, and responsible email sending practices.

Email addresses that request no further communication must not be contacted again.

The domain may not be used to disguise sender identity, bypass spam filters, or distribute malicious or deceptive content.

Abuse reports concerning this domain may be investigated and sending access may be suspended where appropriate.

usenazter.com must not be advertised or promoted through unsolicited commercial email or unsolicited bulk messages.
